## Overall Impression

The flagship forecast path is excellent, specific, and trustworthy — comparable to the quality bar named (Garmin, Flighty, Linear). But "every screen should communicate professionalism" is currently false for most of the app by file count. The biggest opportunity is finishing the rollout the flagship already defines, ahead of any new design exploration. One real safety liability hides in the gap (P0 below).

## What's Working

1. InstrumentGauge.tsx — a disciplined, data-bound signature component; the confidence band and the text confidence pip share one source of truth.
2. The shallow-water advisory (DayDetail.tsx, getShallowWaterConfidence) — computed from real wave/wind thresholds relative to the diver's own configured max depth, delivered as plain-language reasoning.
3. Race-safe async state in AdminPanel.tsx (reqId monotonic counter, issue #169) — engineering discipline ahead of the screen's visual quality.
