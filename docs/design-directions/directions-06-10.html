Five more directions for the forecast screen

A second set, built on the same sample dataset as directions 01 to 05 so you can compare them like for like. Each one leads with a different central device.

  • Hope Cove, South Devon. Baseline clarity of 14.0 m less six penalties gives the visibility shown, in every direction here.
  • Sample data, not a live forecast. The dive history in direction 08 and the reports elsewhere are illustrative, not real people.
  • Interactive: switch days, pick sites, open the breakdown, hit refresh for the loading treatment.
  • Same 4pt spacing scale and type ramp as the first set. Palette, faces, density and the central device are what change.
  • Nothing here inherits any earlier house rules. Each direction is judged on what it does for a diver holding a phone and what it costs to build.
