Five directions for the forecast screen

Same site, same forecast, same sample dataset, drawn five different ways. Pick a direction (or parts of two) and it becomes the basis for the rebuild.

  • Every number below comes from one shared dataset for Hope Cove, South Devon. The arithmetic is real: 14.0 m baseline clarity minus the six penalties equals the visibility shown.
  • Sample data, not a live forecast. Diver reports are illustrative placeholders, not real people.
  • Each mock is interactive. Switch days, open the breakdown, change units, hit refresh to see the loading treatment.
  • All five share one 4pt spacing scale and one type ramp. What changes is palette, face pairing, density and the central device.
  • No direction here inherits the old house rules about dark grounds, no cards or no light themes. Each one is judged on what it does for a diver holding a phone, and on what it costs to build.
